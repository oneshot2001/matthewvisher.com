Flagship · Video provenance

EdgeProof

Built so video can prove it hasn't been altered, from the lens to the courtroom. In development.

  1. 01Lens

    Signed at the source. AXIS cameras sign the video; each detection gets its own record, signed with a software key today and in the camera later.

  2. 02Archive

    Built to survive the transcode. When retention thins the footage, a second record will say what was read, what was written, and how. In spec review.

  3. 03Courtroom

    Anyone should be able to check it. The verifier, in development, walks the chain and gives a graded answer.
