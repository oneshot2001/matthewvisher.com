Flagship · Video provenance

EdgeProof

Video that can prove it hasn't been altered, from the lens to the courtroom.

  1. 01Lens

    Signed at the camera. Every detection gets its own record before anything leaves the device.

  2. 02Archive

    Survives the transcode. When retention thins the footage, a second record says what was read, what was written, and how.

  3. 03Courtroom

    Anyone can check it. A verifier walks the chain and gives a graded answer.
